Norbert

The ghost who turns your life into stats.

Norbert is a nerdy ghost who has followed you since the day you were born, clipboard in hand. He counted everything: the toilet paper rolls, the kilometres walked, the litres of sweat.

Pass the phone around, give a few details about yourself, and bet on the number. Then Norbert reveals the truth, and taunts whoever was furthest off.

The stats are available in English, French, Italian, German, Spanish and Portuguese.

HOW TO PLAY

1. Tell Norbert

The stat asks for a detail or two, like your age or your weight, so the result is really about you. Too personal? Skip to another stat.

2. Bet

Each player in turn enters a guess for the number, without peeking at the others.

3. Boo!

Norbert pops out with the answer, a fun fact to put it in perspective, and a jab at the worst guesser.

Scoring

You score 1 point for every player whose guess was further off than yours. Get really close for a bonus:

Within 10% · +2 Within 1% · +5

Your game, your way

2 to 8 players on a single phone, 5, 10 or 15 rounds. An interrupted game can be resumed later.

101 stats about you

From your whole life down to what your body is doing right now: hair grown, blinks, heartbeats, pizzas eaten… Believable numbers, silly questions.

No ads, no tracking

Norbert is free. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. The app works entirely offline: what you tell Norbert stays on your phone.

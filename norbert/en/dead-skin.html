How many kilos of dead skin have you shed since you were born?

At 30, about 16 kilos

“Enough to fill at least 9.1 vacuum cleaner bags.”

A good part of the dust in your home is you.

How Norbert counts

About 1.5 grams a day. A study of indoor air measured 0.03 to 0.09 gram of dead skin an hour, that is 0.7 to 2.2 grams a day. A vacuum cleaner bag holds 4 liters, and the test dust that imitates household dust weighs 0.45 kg per liter, so 1.8 kg per bag. Skin is lighter than that dust, so it would really take even more bags.

Sources: Weschler et al., Environmental Science & Technology, 2011; Philips et Bosch, fiches produit des aspirateurs (sacs de 4 à 5 L); DMT / TÜV Nord, poussière d'essai type 8 (IEC 60312)

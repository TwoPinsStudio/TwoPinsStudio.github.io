How many steps have you taken since you were born?

At 30, about 52,925,000

“And I counted from the very first one, at around 12 months. You fell right after. I wrote that down too.”

The 10,000-step goal comes from a 1965 Japanese pedometer ad.

How Norbert counts

A study published in Nature in 2017, using the phones of more than 700,000 people in 111 countries, measured an average of about 5,000 steps a day. Norbert counts 5,000 a day from age 1.

Sources: Althoff et al., Nature, 2017

How many times have you blinked since you were born?

At 30, about 157,680,000

“I slip behind you every time you blink. You've never seen me.”

You blink much less while staring at a screen. Hence the dry eyes.

How Norbert counts

About 15 blinks a minute at rest, over 16 waking hours a day. In front of a screen, it drops to 5 to 7 a minute.

Sources: BioNumbers (Harvard University)

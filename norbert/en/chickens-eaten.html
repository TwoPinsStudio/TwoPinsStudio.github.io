How Norbert counts

Norbert counts about 12 kilos of chicken meat per person a year in France, and about 1.5 kilos of meat per chicken, so 8 chickens a year. Careful: the 2025 agricultural statistics give 25.7 kilos per person, but in carcass weight, bones included. With that figure, it would be more like 17 chickens a year.

Sources: Agreste & FranceAgriMer, 2025

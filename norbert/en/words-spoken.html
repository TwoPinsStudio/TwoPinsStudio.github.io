How many words have you spoken since you were born?

At 30, about 163,520,000

“Written down, they'd fill a library. Nobody would borrow a thing.”

The longest word in major English dictionaries: pneumonoultramicroscopicsilicovolcanoconiosis, 45 letters.

How Norbert counts

About 16,000 words a day from age 2. A study published in Science in 2007, using recorders worn by 396 students, measured 16,215 words a day for women and 15,669 for men. The myth that women talk three times more doesn't hold up.

Sources: Mehl, Vazire, Ramírez-Esparza, Slatcher & Pennebaker, Science, 2007

How Norbert counts

The starting figure, about 86 billion neurons, comes from Azevedo and colleagues, in 2009. Norbert then removes about 85,000 a day from age 20. That pace is a calculation based on work by Pakkenberg and colleagues, in 2003: between 20 and 90, the cortex loses about 10 percent of its neurons, which averages about 85,000 a day. Careful: some researchers think the loss is smaller, or even negligible.

Sources: Azevedo et al., Journal of Comparative Neurology, 2009; Pakkenberg & Gundersen, Journal of Comparative Neurology, 1997; Pakkenberg et al., Experimental Gerontology, 2003; Jardim-Messeder et al., Frontiers in Neuroanatomy, 2017

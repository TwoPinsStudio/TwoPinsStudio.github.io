How Norbert counts

The National Sleep Foundation recommends 14 to 17 hours for a newborn, 9 to 11 hours for a school-age child, and 7 to 9 hours for an adult. Norbert uses an average of 9 hours a night over the whole life: that is his estimate.

Sources: Hirshkowitz et al., National Sleep Foundation, Sleep Health, 2015

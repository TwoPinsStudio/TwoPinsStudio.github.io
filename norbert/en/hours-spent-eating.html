How Norbert counts

According to the OECD in 2016, the French spend about 2 hours and 13 minutes a day eating and drinking, against 1 hour and 3 minutes in the United States, for an average of about an hour and a half. Italy and Spain are also above 2 hours. The French statistics office even gave 2 hours and 22 minutes in 2010. Careful: Norbert's values for the United Kingdom and Germany have not been verified.

Sources: OCDE / OECD, Time Use (2016); Insee, Enquête Emploi du temps, 2010

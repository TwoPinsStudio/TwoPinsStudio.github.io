How Norbert counts

About 100,000 hairs on the head, 85 to 90 percent of which are growing at any given time. Each one grows about 0.35 millimetre a day, roughly 12.8 centimetres a year. According to the Fédération Aéronautique Internationale, space begins 100 kilometres up, at the Kármán line.

Sources: StatPearls (National Library of Medicine); Fédération aéronautique internationale (ligne de Kármán)

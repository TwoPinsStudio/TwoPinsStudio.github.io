Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Norbert does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. Nothing you type leaves your phone.
  • The values you enter for a stat (age, height, weight…) are used only to compute the result on your device.
  • Your settings, player names and saved game are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

If you contact me by email, I use your address only to reply to you.

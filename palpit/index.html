Pal & Pit

In a system where order reigns, a gate to the outside world opens. Two programs fight to escape, but only one can get out.

A face-to-face race for two players on a single phone or tablet.

Collect signatures to reach the gate — but careful: the closer you get to your goal, the harder it is to reach.

The story

At the heart of the system where two programs live, a gate that lets them escape opens. It will close once one of them has crossed it, so a race begins. But the system disagrees, and will do everything in its power to quarantine the two warring programs. Luckily, they can count on their neighbours, who have organised a resistance and will do everything to help them reach their goal.

HOW TO PLAY

THE GOAL

Two players, one screen, one line. Pick up signatures: they carry you forward. First one to the win line at the front of the screen wins.

Hit an obstacle and you lose ground. Lose too much and fall off the line, and your program is disintegrated — you lose.

CONTROLS

FLIP

Cross to the other side of the line. The only way past a tall spike is to not be there.

JUMP

Hop over whatever is coming. Hold it down to go higher and further.

FIRE

The round one on your own edge. It spends the boost you are carrying, and it waits until you say so.

BOOSTS

They bob over the line in pairs, one on each side, never the same two. Whoever gets there first gets it — and takes it away from the other. You can only carry one, so a boost in your pocket is a boost you are not picking up.

MULTIPLIER

Every collectible counts twice. Greed, rewarded.

MAGNET

Collectibles come to you. Even the ones on the wrong side of the line.

SHIELD

Takes one hit for you. After that you are on your own again.

LIGHTNING

Reach across the table and knock your opponent backwards, wherever they are.

FREEZE

Your opponent presses. Nothing happens.

INVERT

Your opponent's two controls swap over. They will find out the hard way.

FLATLINE

The line goes quiet. Nothing in anybody's way for a while — including theirs.

Modes

RACE

Two players on one device, face to face. First to the win line gets out; caught by the death line and you lose.

SOLO

The same race on your own. Reach the win line before the death line catches you.

Why I made it

Years ago I played Lub vs Dub, a two-player game on a single screen, and I loved it. It no longer exists, and I missed it.

So I made the game I wanted to play again: quick to start, easy to understand, tense until the last second — two people around one screen, looking at each other.

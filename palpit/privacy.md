# Pal & Pit — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 30, 2026*

Pal & Pit does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No account, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- The game does not connect to the internet.
- Your settings (sound, haptics, difficulty, language) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

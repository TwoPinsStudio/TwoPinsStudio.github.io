# Pal & Pit

Two programs trapped in a dark city. One exit. Only one of them gets out.

Pal & Pit is a race for two players on a single device, sitting face to face. Flip across the line, jump over whatever is coming, grab boosts and throw them at your opponent. First to the win line escapes; the one caught by the death line stays behind. You can also play solo and try to escape the system on your own.

## Why I made it

I wanted a game two people could play on one phone or tablet, on a table, looking at each other rather than at separate screens — quick to start, easy to understand, and tense until the last second.

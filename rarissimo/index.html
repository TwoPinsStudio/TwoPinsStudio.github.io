Rarissimo

The rarest wins.

A category appears: countries of Europe, cheeses, Disney films… Each team secretly agrees on one answer, then everyone reveals at the same time on 3-2-1. Obvious answers barely score. Rare ones pay off.

About 300 categories across 11 themes, from geography to cinema, sport and food. One phone on the table is all you need.

Available in English, French, Italian, German, Spanish and Portuguese, with categories adapted to each language.

HOW TO PLAY

1. Whisper

A category appears. Each team quietly agrees on a single answer.

2. Reveal

On 3-2-1, every team calls out its answer at the same time.

3. Score

The rarer the answer, the more it is worth. A wrong answer scores nothing. A correct answer missing from the list still counts: the table agrees on its value.

Rarity

Easy · 1 pt Medium · 2 pts Rare · 3 pts Legendary · 5 pts

Same answer? Nobody scores

If two teams give the same answer, neither of them scores. Playing it safe has a price.

Final round counts double

The team with the most points after the last round wins. In case of a tie, a sudden-death round decides.

Your game, your way

2 to 4 teams, 5, 10 or 15 rounds, and the themes you like. An interrupted game can be resumed later.

No ads, no tracking

Rarissimo is free. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. Only anonymous answer statistics are shared, to measure how rare each answer really is.
